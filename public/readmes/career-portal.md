# Career & Recruitment Portal

A public-facing recruitment portal for presenting vacancies and guiding candidates through application flows.

## Candidate experience

Visitors can browse available positions, open detailed vacancy pages and move into application forms for professional or internship opportunities. A separate CV submission flow supports candidates who want to register their interest outside a specific vacancy.

## Content structure

The portal combines career information with vacancy records, company information, frequently asked questions and contact routes. Vacancy identifiers are exposed through controlled public URLs rather than direct database identifiers.

## Architecture

Laravel manages vacancy and application workflows. Inertia connects the backend to the React interface so public pages and form flows remain part of one application architecture.

## Engineering focus

The central concern is turning structured recruitment data into a clear public journey: discovery, vacancy review and application. This requires the content-facing interface and backend vacancy records to stay aligned.
